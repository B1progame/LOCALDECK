# Code of conduct

Be kind. Talk about the matter, not about the person. Assume that others mean well, and ask when something is unclear.

Not accepted: insults, harassment, discrimination, sexual remarks, threats, publishing private data of others, and spam. Many people here are at school, so write in a way that suits everyone.

B1progame may edit or delete posts and block people who do not keep to this. Tell them about problems through the address on the [privacy page](https://b1progame.github.io/LOCALDECK/#/privacy). Reports are treated confidentially.
